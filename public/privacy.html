Account verification

Privacy Policy

This policy explains what Oregon State Roleplay collects during Roblox verification and how that information is used.

Effective August 6, 2026

Information we collect

When you verify, we receive your Discord user ID and tag, Roblox user ID, Roblox username, Roblox display name, verification status, and verification time. We also record whether you accepted the server rules.

We never receive or store your Roblox or Discord password.

How we use it

  • Link one Roblox account to one Discord member.
  • Assign verification and onboarding roles.
  • Prevent duplicate or abusive account links.
  • Support moderation, eligibility, and server safety.

Storage and sharing

Verification records are limited to authorized OSRP systems and staff who need them for server operations. We do not sell personal information. Data may be processed by Discord, Roblox, Vercel, and Cloudflare as required to provide the verification service.

Retention and deletion

We retain verification records while they are needed to operate the community, enforce account-linking rules, or resolve moderation matters. You may request access, correction, unlinking, or deletion through the OSRP support channel. Some records may be retained when required for safety, fraud prevention, or enforcement.

Your choices

Verification is optional, but unverified members may not receive full server access. You can revoke the app from your Roblox account settings. Revoking authorization does not automatically remove an existing Discord verification record; contact OSRP support for that request.

Contact and updates

Questions or privacy requests can be submitted through the OSRP Discord support area. We may update this policy when the verification service or legal requirements change. The effective date above will be revised when material changes are made.
